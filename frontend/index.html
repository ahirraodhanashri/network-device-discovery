How it works

Layer 2 · ARPBroadcast frame to ff:ff:ff:ff:ff:ff. Each live host replies with its MAC.
Layer 3 · ICMPEcho request/reply. Reply TTL hints at the OS; timing gives RTT.
Layer 4 · TCPA completed SYN / SYN-ACK / ACK handshake means the port is open.
AnalysisScan-to-scan comparison and duplicate-MAC checks flag possible ARP spoofing.

Limitations: ARP works only inside the local broadcast domain; your own machine does not answer its own ARP request; absent devices may be asleep or isolated; spoofing indicators are hints, not proof.
